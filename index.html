In healthcare, I learned the supreme importance of clarity, high-stakes decision-making, and deep empathy for the user (the patient and the clinician). When I transitioned into software development and UI/UX design, I brought that exact clinical meticulousness with me. Every interface I build prioritizes accessibility, speed, and intuitive ergonomics.
